# Fimbrethil of the Havens, and the line of markers

*Playtest run 3 of the TOR2E Character Tracker (SW `tor2e-v128`, harness 280/280). A solo
session in Strider Mode, played start to finish with a pre-generated hero, pressing only
controls the app offers and finding them by their visible label. 469 driver invocations,
**zero page errors**.*

Everything in `code` or *italic quotation* below is the app's own text, copied out of the
running application. The prose between is mine.

Run 1 built a hero by hand and played one saga. Run 2 played a pregen and re-tested run 1's
sixteen fixes. This run does three things: it plays a saga end to end again, it re-tests all
seventeen run-2 fixes and the thirteen minors **in play** rather than by reading the diff
(GOTCHA 20), and it goes after the surfaces both earlier runs listed as never reached —
Opening Volley, Pierce against an Encounter foe, the Moria Archfoe Duel, Restore-Points
*restore*, Import Character, the print render, and the 4-Flaws retirement.

---

## I. One tap, and a scholar

The app opened on **▶ Play**, which said what it always says and is right to say:

> *"You need a hero before you can play."* — **✨ Give me a ready-made hero** · **🛠️ I'll make my own**

The first-run tutorial offer fired on the way (*"Never played before? The guided tutorial runs
on a throwaway practice hero"*), and I put it off. From the pregen list I took **Fimbrethil of
the Havens** — *Elves of Lindon · Scholar · Str 4 Hrt 3 Wit 7 · End 24 Hope 13* — because she
carries a **Great Bow** and I wanted a hero who would actually shoot something (run 2's Ranger
had Bows 0, which is why half of combat went untested).

☰ menu → **🗡️ Solo Play: Strider Mode**. TNs dropped to `14 / 15 / 11`, Fellowship Rating became
3, the free *Strider* feature appeared, and — correctly, per the 2026-08-24 rulebook pass — the
experience scheme flipped itself to **Milestone** without being asked.

Her patron is `Gilraen, daughter of Dírhael`, with the acute accent that run 2 found matched no
table. It matches now. **▶ Begin — give me a reason to go** went straight to her:

> **🗺️ Begin your saga** — *"What sends your hero out? Your patron Gilraen, daughter of Dírhael
> suggests: **A settlement has come under the eye of the Shadow. Why is this place significant?**"*

I answered: because the folk of Combe keep a stone the Elves set there in the First Age, and
nobody alive remembers what it is for.

Two turns of **👂 Ask around for news** gave me the Lore Table twice:

> *"You listen at the fire and in the doorways. What you hear turns on three things:
> **Break · Broken · Court**."*
> *"…**Chase · Concealed · Death**."*

A broken court. A concealed death, and something still moving. On the Oracle tab, Telling Table
set to **Doubtful**:

> `Q: Is the stone at Combe still whole?` · `Feat die: 7 · chance: doubtful (yes if ≥ 8)` · **→ NO**

Not weathered down, then. Broken.

## II. The road east

I set this journey up on the **Journey tab** rather than from ▶ Play, deliberately, because the
Character tab's own adventure-loop card tells you to: *"Do now: Open the Journey tab, fill in
where you are going, and tap ▶ Start Journey."* Mithlond → Combe, 8 hexes, 2 of them hard
terrain, **Border Land**, **Forced March**, **Mounted** with Vigour 4, and a **Peril rating of 2**
— three things no previous run had exercised.

It played well. Forced March moved her 2–3 hexes a day and charged +2 Travel Fatigue for the
privilege; the peril events arrived tagged `⚠️ [Peril]` independently of the hex path; and
every event brought its own roll, which is run 1's finding 2 fixed and still fixed:

> `▶ Ill Choices — roll Awareness` · `Awareness ◆◆◇◇◇◇ vs TN 14 · hard terrain −1d`
> `On a failure, the app applies the event's cost for you.`
> `🎲 Roll Awareness` · `Skip this roll`

She failed it, and the app did what run 2 said it would not:

> `▶ Awareness roll for Ill Choices — 6 vs TN 14: failure — +1 Shadow (0 → 1)`

A Despair event later did the same. Eye Awareness climbed from the Eye icons on the Marching
Tests — *"Raise by 1 for any Eye icon outside combat"* now genuinely fires on Journey rolls, not
only Dice-tab ones.

Arrival was honest about its cost:

> *"Journey complete! Travel Fatigue accumulated: 13. Mount Vigour 4: −4 → 9. Arrival TRAVEL roll:
> FAIL (Feat 1, total 9 vs Heart TN 15) → no reduction. Lingering 9 Fatigue added to character
> Fatigue (0 → 9)."*

Nine days on the road; `Day 1` → `Day 8` on the character sheet, which is run 2's minor fixed.

And then the first thing that got in the way. The ▶ Play tab, which had been told nothing about
any of this, still read **AT YOUR HOME — *"You are somewhere safe. Nothing is trying to kill you
yet."*** and offered **🥾 Set out on the road**. I pressed it, to see, and it cheerfully asked me
where I was going and replaced the whole finished journey with a fresh 4-hex walk — destination,
Border Land, forced march, mount and peril all gone, no warning *(Finding 2)*. I restored the
snapshot and got in through the side door instead: pressing **▶ We set out — begin the journey**
on the Character tab *after arriving* finally told Play what had happened, and the recovery the
run-1 fix added appeared exactly as designed:

> **ON THE ROAD TO COMBE, IN BREE-LAND** — *"The road is behind you — you finished this journey on
> the Journey tab. Tap **We have arrived** to carry on."*

## III. Combe, which will not say the word

> **AT COMBE, IN BREE-LAND** — *"You have arrived. This is where the thing you came for is — or is not."*

**👀 Look around** gave me *"Hinder · Growing · Legend"*, which is a good answer: the village has
built a story on top of the stone, and the story is in the way. **🔮 Ask a yes/no question** now
lets you set the odds — run 2's minor, fixed — so I asked at **Doubtful** whether anyone would
talk about the stone. **NO.** I asked at Middling whether the smith's daughter was still in the
village. **YES.** Both landed in the Chronicle with the apostrophe intact and the sentences
separated, which is run 2's finding 12 fixed:

> `Q: Is the smith's daughter still in the village? · Telling Table (Middling) → YES`
> `You wonder: Is the smith's daughter still in the village? (middling) The answer is YES.`

**🎯 Try something → 📜 Remember a tale of this** rolled her best skill and it worked:
`Lore roll 20 vs 11: it works.` She knows the stone: not a boundary-mark, a grave-marker, one of a
line, and the line runs east.

Making the elders say so took a **Council** — Resistance **Bold (6)**, audience **Reluctant −1d**.
She opened with AWE and failed, which set the Time Limit to 3, which made the whole thing
unwinnable, which is the correct shape of a bad council. Three attempts, one success, and then:

> *"⏳ Time limit reached. 1 / 6 successes. Choose outcome:"*
> **Accept Failure (refused outright)** · **Success with Woe (achieve the goal at a price)**

The *"LM approval"* run 1 complained about is gone. She took the Woe: they gave her the name, and
she owes Combe something she has not been told the shape of.

Then a **Skill Endeavour** at **Hazardous** risk to open the grave — Resistance 6, Time Limit 4.
The first failed roll auto-applied its consequence and said so (`⚠️ Failure-with-Woe auto-applied:
+2 Fatigue (now 11)`), she scored 4 of 6, and it closed as *Failure with Woe*: the turf off, the
slab bare, and the night gone.

She went back the next night and forced it, at **Foolish** risk. Foolish risk means a single
failed roll ends everything, and hers did. What the app showed for that was nothing at all — the
endeavour simply vanished and the log read `No attempts yet.` *(Finding 6)*. The sentence
`💀 DISASTER on a Foolish endeavour — it fails and cannot be resumed` exists; it is written into
`char.skillEndeavour.rolls` and then wiped off the screen a millisecond later.

## IV. What came up out of the ground

**⚔️ Something attacks!** from the Play tab opened the bestiary with a legend I had not seen
before — *"An attack like 'sword 2d (5/16)' = 2 dice, Damage 5, Injury 16"* — and I took a
**Barrow-wight** (End 20, Parry 5, Armour 2, Might 2).

First I tested the thing run 2 caught the app lying about. From **🗡️ Skirmish** stance I tried to
attack with the Cudgel:

> **⚠️ Cannot attack from Skirmish** — *"Cudgel is a melee weapon, and from Skirmish stance melee
> weapons cannot attack at all — you are too far back to reach the foe. Your options: attack with
> a ranged weapon instead, or step into a closer stance. **Switch to Open stance now?**"*

Blocked, explained, and offered a way out. The Great Bow shot fine from the same stance, with the
penalty shown: `You · Great Bow · 6 vs TN 19 (14 Str + Parry 5) → FAIL · miss [Skirmish −1d]`.

TN 19 is a hard number for a scholar with two ranks of Bows, so she went **Forward** and spent
Hope on every shot. Four hits out of seven, four Endurance a time, and a Piercing Blow on a
Gandalf rune that the wight shrugged off (`Piercing Blow — foe Protection ★ vs 18 → resisted`).
Its Cold grip found a gap on the way back and the app rolled her Protection for her, and then:

> *"Wound Severity: **Grievous Injury 👁 — Unconscious & Dying (as if Wounded twice)**"*

That is the worst result on the app's own wound table, and it is where this run's most
disagreeable finding lives. Nothing happened. No Dying pill, no banner, no timer. The ▶ Play tab
kept offering the same five choices, and when I pressed the one it had promoted to the top —
**🩹 Tend the wound — *"A HEALING roll — a Wound will not rest off."*** — it answered:

> *"No Severe Injury day-count to reduce. (Moderate/Grievous injuries do not use day-tracking.)"*

No roll. Not once, not ever. She fought on unconscious and dying, killed the wight in the fourth
volley, and stayed Wounded for the rest of the campaign *(Finding 4)*.

The fight folded itself into one Chronicle heading — `⚔️ Combat vs Barrow-wight — 1 foe · 1 slain ·
2 rounds · −8 End · Wounded · -6 Hope` — and into the Campaign Timeline, which run 2 found empty
and which is now full.

Before I left the barrow I tested the two escape routes. **🏃 Fly, You Fools!** now offers two real
buttons instead of asking you to type a digit, and the Defensive one rolls in place with the foe's
Parry in the TN, which is run 2's finding 11 exactly fixed:

> `You · escape attempt · Great Bow · 8 vs TN 17 (14 Str + Parry 3) · Def −1d → FAIL · you remain engaged`

The Rearward one sets your stance and says *"On your next action you may escape without a roll"* —
and there is no control anywhere that performs that escape, ever *(Finding 8)*.

The barrow paid: a **Greater** hoard, tainted, which defaulted the party size to **1** (run 2's
finding 14, fixed) and yielded 8 Treasure. From the **Treasure Index** I took the Horn of Boromir's
stat line, renamed it *The Silver-Tipped Horn of the Barrow*, and ticked **⚠️ Cursed — Shadow Taint**.
The Greed test failed:

> *"Wisdom Test: FAIL (Feat 8, 0 ✦ vs Wits TN 11). Reduction: −0. Net Shadow gain: 1 (2 → 3).
> ⚠️ CURSED (Shadow Taint). You will gain +1 Shadow each Fellowship Phase while bearing this item."*

She kept it. That turns out to be the whole story.

**✅ Our business here is done** → **🥾 Set out for home** → *"The way we came"* → a 9-hex return.
Run 1's finding 11 is genuinely fixed: the road home can be travelled now, in the same units as
the road out (`2 stretches in 2 days; you are 4 of 9 of the way`), and a Gandalf rune reads as

right past them, both times, all the way home *(Finding 1)*. The outbound leg surfaces them

one of the two legs.

**🏠 We are safe again** moved the loop to the Fellowship Phase — and left `char.journey.active`
true, so the Journey tab spent the rest of the campaign displaying an in-progress journey to a
place called `home` with an unresolved event waiting at hex 8.

## VI. Four Fellowship Phases

This is the part run 2 could not reach at all, because the phase it fixed could only be run once.
It can be run four times now. I ran four.

**Phase 1, Ordinary.** Step 2 previewed everything before committing anything — *"Current: 7 / 13
→ will become 10 (+3)"* and *"⚠️ Shadow Taint: 1 cursed item (The Silver-Tipped Horn of the Barrow)
→ +1 Shadow this phase"*. I closed the app between step 1 and step 2 to check, and reopening put
me back on step 2 with the phase type intact. Applying Recovery a second time was refused. Step 3
opened **Spend Skill Points** *above* the wizard — `z-index 240` against the wizard's 200, and
**19 of 19 buttons hit-testable**, which is run 2's finding 2 dead. Step 4 took *Ponder Storied
and Figured Maps* (free for a Scholar) and *Write a Song*, and I wrote her one:
*The Lay of the Broken Marker — "Of a court that does not sit, and a name Combe would not say."*

Inside that phase I bought a Valour rank, and the picker that grants the Reward opened. I closed
the app on it, the way you do. On the next load the Character tab carried a standing control:

> *"You have earned 1 Reward that has not been chosen yet. Ranks of Valour grant a Reward; ranks of
> Wisdom grant a Virtue."* — **🎁 Choose your Reward**

That is run 2's finding 3 fixed, and it is a good fix. It is also only half the path. Claiming the
Reward opens a second dialog — *"Apply Keen to… Great Bow / Cudgel / Cancel"* — and **Cancel** at
that step decrements the owed counter, applies nothing, and leaves you with no Reward and nothing
owed *(Finding 3)*. I lost two Rewards this way, 16 Adventure Points for nothing, before I worked
out what was happening.

The same phase also sold me a **second Valour rank** — Valour 1 → 2 → 3 in one Fellowship Phase —
while the Wisdom row beside it was locked with *"Already raised Valour this Fellowship Phase —
Valour and Wisdom are exclusive per phase (RAW p.119)"* and the card above listed *"Max 1 rank per
Skill per phase"* *(Finding 7)*.

**Phase 2, Yule.** Age 135 → 136, +7 Skill Points from Wits, all Hope back, the year rolled to
2966. **Heal Scars** took the Shadow Scar she had bought with Harden Will; **Raise an Heir** made
Faelivrin with 3 Previous Experience for 3 AP and 3 Treasure. Both Yule-only undertakings work.

**Phases 3 and 4** were routine, which is the point: `phasesCompleted: 4`, `fpWizardState: null`
after each, Eye Awareness reset to its starting value each time, Strengthen Fellowship raising
Fellowship Rating to 4 and then correctly expiring one phase later.

What four Fellowship Phases did not do was heal her. After all of them she was still
`Wounded`, still carrying `Grievous Injury 👁 — Unconscious & Dying`, still at 16/24 Endurance
with 11 Fatigue. The only thing in the app that clears a Grievous wound is un-ticking the
Wounded checkbox yourself.

# Findings

Ranked by how much they got in the way. Every quotation is the app's own text.

### 1 — The road home has no Journey Events. The fix reached one leg of the journey. *(dead end in the fiction)*
**Beat V.** `_playStepChoices()` gained a `_playEventDue()` branch for `case 'journey'`
(`src/04-render.js:3961-3966`) and **`case 'home'` did not** (`:3997-4001`). The return leg is a
real journey — `playSetOutHome()` builds one, `playTravel()` advances it through
`applyMarchingTestResult()`, which schedules `nextEventHex` exactly as it does outbound. I
travelled 9 hexes home with `nextEventHex: 7` and then `8` sitting on the character, and Play
offered *"🥾 Travel onward"* every single time. On arrival, `playGoStep('fellowship')` left
`char.journey.active === true`, so the Journey tab was still showing `Combe, in Bree-land → home ·
8 / 8 hexes · NEXT EVENT hex 8` for the rest of the campaign.

Half of every adventure's travel — the return road the app added a whole control for — has no
pressure in it.

### 2 — A journey started on the Journey tab is invisible to ▶ Play, which then silently destroys it. *(silent state change)*
**Beat II.** The Character tab's adventure-loop card says *"Do now: Open the Journey tab, fill in
where you are going, and tap ▶ Start Journey."* Doing that leaves `char.saga.step` on `'haven'`,
because only `playSetOut()` advances it. So with an 8-hex mounted forced march to Combe live and
0/8 covered, ▶ Play read:

> **AT YOUR HOME** — *"You are somewhere safe. Nothing is trying to kill you yet."*
> **🥾 Set out on the road** — *"Begin the journey to wherever this takes you."*

Pressing it asked *"Where are you going?"*, asked how far, and **overwrote the live journey with no
confirmation** (`src/04-render.js:4165`): destination, 8 hexes, Border Land, Forced March, Mounted
+ Vigour 4 and Peril 2 replaced by a 4-hex wild-land walk. The Journey tab's "Journey in Progress"
card was now describing a different journey.

Recovery exists but reads as nonsense: you press **▶ We set out — begin the journey** on the
Character tab *after you have arrived*, and it answers *"Do now: Open the Journey tab, fill in where
you are going, and tap ▶ Start Journey."*

### 3 — A Reward you earned is destroyed if you cancel the "Apply to…" step. *(described capability, no control)*
**Beat VI.** Run 2's finding 3 is fixed one level deep: abandoning the **🎖️ Pick a New Reward**
picker now leaves a standing *"You have earned 1 Reward that has not been chosen yet"* +
**🎁 Choose your Reward** on the Character tab, and it survives a reload. But `pickNewReward()`
decrements the counter **before** the apply step runs:

```js
function pickNewReward(name) {
  closeNewReward();
  char.pendingRewards = Math.max(0, (parseInt(char.pendingRewards) || 0) - 1);
  saveCharacter();
  promptApplyReward(name, 'new');      // ← cancel here and the Reward is gone
}
```
`src/05-combat-build.js:2508-2512`

Reproduced twice, once by closing the app on the *"Apply Keen to…"* dialog and once by pressing its
own **Cancel**: `pendingRewards → 0`, `char.rewards` unchanged, `#owed-picks` hidden after reload.
Two Valour ranks (16 AP) bought and neither Reward received.

### 4 — The worst wound in the game has no effect, no countdown, and the control offered for it refuses every time. *(dead end in the fiction)*
**Beat IV, and every beat after it.** `rollWoundSeverity()` returns
`{ label: 'Grievous Injury 👁', detail: 'Unconscious & Dying (as if Wounded twice)', days: 0 }`
(`src/05-combat-build.js:930`). The app then does nothing with it: no Dying state, no prompt, no
timer, the same five ▶ Play choices, and full agency — she shot a bow the same second.

Because `days: 0`, `rollFirstAid()` refuses (`src/05-combat-build.js:153`), and ▶ Play's
**🩹 Tend the wound** — the choice the app *promotes to the top of the list while you are Wounded*,
captioned *"A HEALING roll — a Wound will not rest off."* — answers, forever:

> *"No Severe Injury day-count to reduce. (Moderate/Grievous injuries do not use day-tracking.)"*

Four Fellowship Phases did not clear it either. The only route out of a Grievous or Moderate wound
in the whole app is un-ticking the Wounded checkbox by hand, which nothing tells you.

### 5 — A number the Bout of Madness did not offer silently clears all your Shadow and gives you no Flaw. *(silent refusal, on the most consequential prompt in the game)*
**Beat VII.** The prompt numbers the remaining Flaws by their *original* index, so on a second bout
it reads:

> *"Pick a Flaw to add (only un-owned shown): **2.** Scornful **4.** Traitorous. Enter the number —
> or Cancel to skip and just clear Shadow."*

Typing `1` (or `3`, or junk) falls straight through the guard and the next two lines run regardless:

```js
if (n >= 1 && n <= 4 && flaws[n-1] && !flawsField.includes(flaws[n-1])) { …add flaw… }
```

## Two notes on method

**Probe artifacts I chased and discarded**, recorded so the next run does not chase them again:
- **One new `tor2e-char-*` slot appeared per page load** — 378 orphan slots against a 4-hero roster.
  That was *my driver*: `tests/browser.js` does its own `goto()` with empty storage before the
  state-restoring init script is installed, so the app builds a blank hero on that first load and
  the restore did not clear it. Fixed by clearing before re-applying the store. Nothing wrong with
  the app.
- **Every control on the Dice tab reported un-hit-testable** — an unrelated `styled-modal-overlay`
  from an unfinished tutorial exit was still open. Always check `occludedBy` before believing a
  hit-test failure.
- **Every Chronicle scene showed the same date** — scenes carry `sc.date`, not `sc.clock`, and
  `journal.scenes` is stored **oldest-first**. Run 2's driver reversed it and read the wrong key.
  The Tale of Years is fine.
- **The War Gear table looked empty** and **the `ch-month` picker looked to be missing Foreyule** —
  `innerText` does not return `<input value>`, and my own output truncation. Both fine.

**What the driver gained this run**, and why: a real **hit-test** (`elementFromPoint` at three
points, after scrolling into view, reporting what occludes) so that "the button is there" and
"a finger can press it" stop being the same claim — that distinction is what GOTCHA 20 is about,
and it is what proved findings 3, 5 and 2 of run 2 genuinely fixed rather than cosmetically fixed;
a `near "<row text>" "<button>"` step, because Spend XP renders every button as a bare `N pts` and
a player tells them apart by the skill name printed beside it; a `doin "<card>" "<label>"` step,
because two cards on the Combat tab both offer a bare **Pick**; and `grabfile`/`upload` so export
and import could be round-tripped through the app's own file controls rather than reimplemented.
