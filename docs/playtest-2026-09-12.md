# Orodreth of the North — a solo session in the TOR2E Tracker

*A playtest of the TOR2E Character Tracker, played as a single session of solo play
(Strider Mode), start to finish, pressing only controls the app actually offers.*

Every roll, oracle answer and system message quoted below in `code` or *italic quotation*
is the app's own text, copied verbatim from the running application. Nothing mechanical
has been invented. The prose between them is mine — which is exactly the division of
labour the app asks for when it tells you, on the very first screen of a new saga:

> **1.** Decide where your hero is right now, and say what they do first.
> **2.** When the world has to answer something you don't know, ask the **Oracle**. When
> your hero attempts something that could fail, **roll it**.
> **3.** Write down what happened in the **Chronicle**, in your own words.

---

## I. Making a man to send out

The app opened on a tab called **▶ Play**, which is a good instinct — a character sheet
is not a game — and it said so plainly: *"You need a hero before you can play. That takes
about a minute."* It offered **✨ Give me a ready-made hero** or **🛠️ I'll make my own**.
I took the long road.

**The Build tab** keeps a live checklist at the top: *"The Build tab does most of character
creation for you. This ticks itself off as you go — 0 of 11 done."* That is a good piece of
design; you never have to wonder what is left.

I picked **Rangers of the North**, which the app previewed before committing anything:

> `Kings of Men — Add +1 to one Attribute. Weakness: only ½ Heart Hope recovered during
> Fellowship Phase (not Yule)` · `Standard: Frugal · Age: 20-50` ·
> `End: Str+20 · Hope: Hrt+6 · Parry: Wits+14` · `Profs: Spears OR Swords (2) + 1 other`

Attribute Set 3 — `Str 6 · Hrt 5 · Wit 3`. On applying, a second dialog appeared behind the
confirmation: **👑 Kings of Men — *Rangers add +1 to one Attribute. Choose which:*** I put it
into **Wits**, wanting a watchman rather than a brawler. Wits 4, Parry 18.

Calling: **Warden**. The app filled in the rest itself — Shadow Path *Path of Despair*,
distinctive feature *Shadow-Lore*, favoured skills *Awareness* and *Healing*. Patron:
**Gilraen, daughter of Dirhael**, whose entry reads *"Resolve Journey Events within Arnor
as if in a Border Land. Meet Patron undertaking grants a rumour"* — and, less
encouragingly, *"Fellowship bonus +0 added."*

Ten Previous Experience points went into **Explore ◆◆◆◆** (a ranger who knows the land
better than the men who live on it) and **Scan ◆◆**. Favoured: **Lore** from the culture,
*Awareness* and *Healing* from the Calling. Features: **Secretive** and **True-hearted**.
Useful item: **Detailed maps** (+1d Explore).

Gear, on the Combat tab: a **Spear** (Dmg 4, Inj 14/16), a **Dagger**, a **Bow**, a
**Leather Corslet** (2d Protection) and a **helm**. Starting Reward **Fell** on the spear
(+2 Injury). Starting Virtue **Prowess**, which opened its own small dialog — *"Prowess —
Lower which TN?"* — and took a point off his Wits TN.

The random-name roller wanted a gender first (*"No Gender set — pick a random name from
either list?"*), then gave me **Orodreth**. Age 37. Safe Haven: **Bree**.

> `The Build tab does most of character creation for you. This ticks itself off as you go
> — 11 of 11 done.`

Then, from the ☰ menu, under a heading the app labels **PLAY ON YOUR OWN (NO GAME MASTER)**:
**🗡️ Solo Play: Strider Mode.** The dialog explains itself better than most rulebooks do:

> *"Normally one player is the Loremaster, who describes the world and decides what
> happens. In Strider Mode you play both parts: you act as your hero, and you ask the
> Oracle whenever you don't know what the world does."*

Target Numbers dropped to `18 − Rating`, the Previous Experience budget went from 10 to 15
(I spent the extra on **Bows ◆◆** and **Song ◆**), Fellowship Rating became 3, and a free
Distinctive Feature appeared: *Strider — Inspired on all Skill rolls while journeying.*

*(One casualty, which I did not notice until much later: the Prowess −1 was silently
recalculated away by the mode switch. See Finding 3.)*

## II. A reason to leave home

The **▶ Play** tab now said: *"Your story hasn't started — You have a hero. Now they need a
reason to leave home."* One button: **▶ Begin — give me a reason to go**.

The app went to the Patron for the premise, which is the right place to go:

> **🗺️ Begin your saga** — *"What sends your hero out? Your patron Gilraen, daughter of
> Dirhael suggests: **A Ranger has gone missing. What has become of them?**"*

I kept it. A Ranger does not simply go missing; Rangers are the ones who find things.
Orodreth knew the man, or knew of him, which in the North is the same debt.

Then **📖 Start a session** on the Character tab opened Session 1, and the Play tab settled
into its first situation:

> **AT BREE** — *"You are somewhere safe. Nothing is trying to kill you yet."*

I asked around twice. The Lore Table answered in its three-word way:

> *"You listen at the fire and in the doorways. What you hear turns on three things:
> **Remove · Peaceful · Refuge**."*

> *"You listen at the fire and in the doorways. What you hear turns on three things:
> **Threaten · Unnatural · Trust**."*

A peaceful refuge, removed. Something unnatural eating at trust. In Bree that reads as:
a wayside shelter the Rangers keep has gone quiet, and nobody in the common room will
say so out loud, because saying so out loud would mean admitting they had noticed.

I took the question to the **Oracle tab** properly, with the Telling Table set to *Middling*:

> **Q:** Is the missing Ranger still alive?
> **Feat die:** `ᚱ` · chance: middling (yes if ≥ 6)
> **→ YES (extreme result or twist — Gandalf Rune)**

Alive, and a rune on top of it. Whatever has happened to him, it is not the ordinary
North-country ending of a body in a ditch.

To be sure I had the shape of it, I rolled a **📜 Patron Quest** — and the app handed me the
exact mission the rumours had been circling:

> *"Patron Quest from Gilraen, daughter of Dirhael (Success die: 6) — **We've received no
> word from a remote Ranger refuge. What fate has befallen our allies?**"*

Two independent oracle rolls converging on one answer is the kind of thing that makes solo
play work. Orodreth saddled up.

---

## III. The road to the Weather Hills

**🥾 Set out on the road.** Destination: *the Ranger refuge in the Weather Hills*.
Distance: *"A fair way — several days."*

> **ON THE ROAD TO THE RANGER REFUGE IN THE WEATHER HILLS** — *"You have covered 0 of 4
> stretches. Day 0."*

I moved over to the **Journey tab**, which had picked the journey up automatically, and
made a Marching Test:

> `Day 4, hex 4: 🚶 Marching Test — Success (Feat 8, total 16, 1 ✦, vs Heart TN 13).
> Advanced 4 hexes in 4 days. Event resolves at hex 4.`

Four days of the Greenway and the old road east, gone in one roll. *(A single successful
Marching Test covers `3 + icons` hexes, and the Play tab had set the whole journey at 4 —
see Finding 5.)*

The road threw one thing at him before the end:

> `Day 4, hex 4: 🎲 Short Cut (Feat 9, region wild). Roll: Awareness. +1 Travel Fatigue.
> If the skill roll succeeds: −1 day to journey`
> `Helpful tracks — follow the tracks`
> `Sub-event roll: 3 · Skill: Awareness`
> `▶ Roll Awareness.`

Bootprints in the mud of a game-trail, too heavy for a deer and too careful for an orc.
The app told me to roll Awareness and gave me nowhere to do it *(Finding 2)*, so I went to
the **Dice tab** and rolled it by hand:

> `Awareness · vs TN 12 — SUCCESS` · *Great Success* · `1 success icon`
> `⚠️ Inspired (Strider (Journey)) — no Hope spent, so no bonus this roll`

Feat 10 (kept from a Favoured pair of 10 and 3), success dice 1 and 6, total 17. He
followed the tracks and they saved him half a day — although nothing in the app records
that they did.

Arrival:

> *"Journey complete! Travel Fatigue accumulated: 1. Arrival TRAVEL roll: SUCCESS (Feat 4,
> 1 ✦, total 13 vs Heart TN 13) → −1 → 0. Lingering 0 Fatigue added to character Fatigue."*

He came up the shoulder of the hill in the late afternoon, and nobody challenged him.

---

## IV. The refuge

The Play tab had lost track of the journey entirely at this point *(Finding 1)* — it still
believed he was standing at the roadside with his boots on. I had to go to the Character
tab's adventure-loop card and press **▶ We have arrived** before the game would move.

> **AT THE RANGER REFUGE IN THE WEATHER HILLS** — *"You have arrived. This is where the
> thing you came for is — or is not."*

**👀 Look around:**

> *"You take the place in. What stands out: **Explore · Far-reaching · History**."*

Older than the Rangers who use it, then. Something under the hill rather than on it — a
Númenórean footing with a woodsman's hut sitting on top of it like a hat on a skull.

**🔍 Search the place:**

> `🔍 Search the place — Scan roll 9 vs 14: it doesn't. (1 ✦)`

Nothing in the upper room but cold ash and a bench that had been moved. The app's advice
after a failure is the best single line of instruction in it: *"Say what goes wrong. A
failure should cost something or change the situation — it is not just 'nothing happens'."*
So: the bench had been moved *recently*, and he had been standing in the doorway long
enough for anyone below to hear the floor.

**🔮 Ask a yes/no question:**

> *"You wonder: Is the missing Ranger still somewhere in this refuge? The answer is **YES**."*

**🤫 Move unseen:**

> `🤫 Move unseen — Stealth roll 7 vs 14: it doesn't.`

He went down the stair carefully and it made no difference at all. Something below had
been listening to the floorboards longer than he had.

---

## V. The fight on the stair

**⚔️ Something attacks!** — and the app opened its bestiary, 56 adversaries with an
inline legend for the notation. I took an **Orc Soldier** (End 12, Parry 3, Armour 2,
Might 1) and an **Orc Guard** (End 18, Parry 4, Armour 3) and set Orodreth **Forward**,
which in hindsight was the mistake of the evening.

Round 1:

> `You · Spear · 14 vs TN 15 (12 Str + Parry 3) → FAIL · miss [Fwd +1d]`
> `You · Spear · 20 vs TN 15 (12 Str + Parry 3) → SUCCESS (2✦) · −4 End → 8/12 [Fwd +1d]`
> `Orc Soldier · Broad-bladed sword · 19 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −5 End → 21/26`
> `Orc Guard · Orc-axe · 16 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → miss`

Round 2:

> `You · Spear · 15 vs TN 15 → SUCCESS · −4 End → 4/12 [Fwd +1d]`

Round 3 was the bad one. Forward stance hands the enemy a die as well as taking one,
and two orcs in a stairwell is two dice:

> `You · Spear · 14 vs TN 15 → FAIL · miss`
> `Orc Soldier · Broad-bladed sword · ★ vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −5 End
> → 11/26 · Piercing Blow — Protection 19 vs 16 → resisted`
> `Orc Guard · Orc-axe · 20 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −6 End → 5/26`

A Gandalf rune on an orc's feat die, and the point of the broad-bladed sword found the
lacing of his corslet and turned on a rib. Five Endurance left, a man he came to save
somewhere below him in the dark, and two orcs between.

He spent Hope and swung Defensive, which cost him a die per foe and produced `4 vs TN 15`.
Then he went Forward again and spent Hope again, because there was nothing else left:

> `You · Spear · 18 vs TN 15 → SUCCESS · −4 End → 0/12 · ⚔ Orc Soldier slain!`

And the Guard answered:

> `Orc Guard · Orc-axe · 23 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −6 End → 0/26 ·
> ⚠ you are Dying · Piercing Blow — Protection 18 vs 18 → resisted`

**Dying.** His Protection roll met the Injury number exactly — 18 against 18 — so the axe
did not open him, it only put him down. The app said nothing further about what Dying
meant, and offered nothing to do about it *(Finding 4)*; the ▶ Play tab, on the next
screen, listed his Endurance as `0/26` and cheerfully offered *"👀 Look around"* as though
nothing had happened.

He used **🏃 Fly, You Fools!**, which explains both RAW escapes and then does the
bookkeeping for you:

> *"Stance set to Rearward. On your next action, you may escape without a roll (no Combat
> Task or attack possible)."*

He went up the stair backwards with the spear between him and the dark, got out, and lay
in the heather above the refuge until the shaking stopped.

> *"🌙 Prolonged Rest applied. A new day dawns (Day 2). Endurance: +26 → 26 / 26"*

## VI. Digging through

The stair was held; the vault was not the only way in. The **Skill Endeavour** tracker
on the Council tab took the job: *Dig out the collapsed stair to the refuge's lower vault*,
Resistance **Laborious (6)**, Time Limit **5**, Risk **Hazardous**.

> `#1 Athletics +1 — Feat 8, total 19 vs STR TN 12, 0 ✦. Contributed +1. SUCCESS (roleplay +1d)`
> `#2 Athletics +1 — Feat ᚱ, total ★ vs STR TN 12, 0 ✦. Contributed +1. SUCCESS`
> `#3 Athletics +1 — Feat 10, total 18 vs STR TN 12, 0 ✦. Contributed +1. SUCCESS`
> `#4 Explore +3 — Feat 4, total 27 vs WIT TN 14, 2 ✦. Contributed +3. SUCCESS (roleplay +2d)`

Three shifts of moving stone with a spear-haft for a lever, and then the thing that
actually worked: he stopped digging and started *reading* the building — the Númenórean
footing had a drain, and drains go somewhere.

> **End of Endeavour** — *"🎉 SUCCESS — accumulated 6 successes (Resistance 6). Task
> completed."*

He came up inside the lower vault behind the orcs, and found the man he had come for.

---

## VII. The argument

The missing Ranger was alive, as the Telling Table had promised, and he would not come.
The **Council** tracker took it: *Persuade the wounded Ranger to abandon the refuge and
come back to Bree*. Resistance **Bold (6)**, Audience **Reluctant (−1d)**.

It went badly from the first word.

> `#1 Awe INTRO — Feat 1, total 1 vs STR TN 12, 0 ✦. Attitude reluctant (-1d). Time Limit set to 3. FAIL`
> `#2 Persuade — Feat 8, total 8 vs WIT TN 14, 0 ✦. Attitude reluctant (-1d). 🤝 Support +1d. No contribution. FAIL`
> `#3 Insight — Feat 10, total 10 vs HRT TN 13, 0 ✦. Attitude reluctant (-1d). No contribution. FAIL (roleplay +1d)`
> `#4 Enhearten — Feat 2, total 2 vs HRT TN 13, 0 ✦. Attitude reluctant (-1d). No contribution. FAIL`

Orodreth is Secretive and Stern and has a Wits TN of 14 and no Persuade at all, and it
showed. A failed opening roll cut the argument to three exchanges, and he lost all three.

> **End of Council** — *"⏳ Time limit reached. 0 / 6 successes. Choose outcome:"* →
> **Accept Failure (refused outright)**

The man had held the refuge for eleven days with a broken arm and he was not going to be
talked out of it by someone who had arrived that afternoon.

## VIII. What it cost

Back on the **Dice tab**, a Shadow Test against the whole weight of it:

> `Shadow Test: Greed (Wisdom) · vs TN 14 — SUCCESS` — *"🌑 Greed test: Reduce incoming
> Shadow by 1 (1 + 0 icons)"*

Two Shadow incoming, one resisted, one taken. And the Eye rose with it — in solo play the
app raises **Eye Awareness** by every point of Shadow gained, which is the mechanism that
makes a solo campaign feel hunted rather than merely difficult. Eye Awareness climbed; the
header pill ticked up; and then:

> **👁️ Revelation Episode** — *"Revelation Episode (Feat 8) — **Cursed possession** — An
> item you carry holds a curse, or is hunted. Play this out. Once it is resolved, Eye
> Awareness resets and the tally begins again."*

Which was awkward, because he had just been through the vault.

The **Hoard Roller** on the Gear tab gave a **Greater** hoard — *"Treasure rolls: 5, 1 = 6
per hero"* — and no magical find (*"No magical treasure this hoard. The riches are mundane
gold and gems."*). But the Revelation had already named the thing, so I added it by hand:

> *"✨ Marvellous Artefact **'The Warden's Ring of Amon Sûl'** added. Load: +1. ⚠️ CURSED
> (Marked). Your bearing of this item draws the Enemy's notice (Eye Awareness +1,
> narrative)."*

Númenórean work, a Blessing of **Awareness**, and cold to the touch. The app proved the
Blessing immediately on the next roll:

> `Awareness · vs TN 12 — SUCCESS` · `Modifier: [✨ The Warden's Ring of Amon Sûl Blessing +2d]`

He should have left it in the vault. He did not.

## IX. The Fellowship Phase

Back at Bree, the app's **Advancement** card is laid out in the order you actually use it —
① earn, ② pools, ③ spend, ④ end the phase. Milestones, per Strider Mode's own advice
against session XP, came from the picker: *Survive a dangerous combat*, *Complete a
meaningful journey*, *Overcome a tricky obstacle*, *Participate in a Council*, *Face a
Revelation Episode*.

Enhearten bought up to ◆ for 4 SP — the failed Council teaching its lesson. Then Valour,
for 8 AP, and the app immediately did the thing it is supposed to do:

> **🎖️ Pick a New Reward** — *"You've gained a new Valour rank — choose a Reward!"* →
> **Grievous** → applied to the **Spear** (Dmg 4 → 5).

The **Fellowship Phase wizard** ran its four steps. Recovery:

> *"✅ +2 Hope (9 → 11) · −1 Shadow (Shadow: 4 → 3)"*

Step 3 turns out to be a signpost rather than a step — it tells you to close the wizard,
spend XP elsewhere, and come back, and it promises *"(Phase state is preserved.)"*, which is
not true *(Finding 6)*. Step 4 is the good one: ten undertakings with their Calling-free
markers, and the solo rewording actually working —

> **Gather Rumours** `FREE — Warden` — *"Pick up a rumour — about a person, place, or
> coming event. **With no Loremaster, ask the Oracle** (Telling for a yes/no, Lore for
> open-ended) and write down what you hear."*

Gather Rumours and **Ponder Storied and Figured Maps**. The phase closed; Eye Awareness
reset to its starting value (*"+2 culture"* — Dúnedain are noticed); Shadow to 0; Hope full.

## X. Under the Mountain (a digression, honestly reported)

I switched the app into **⛏️ Moria Solo Mode** to exercise the second solo campaign. The
fiction here is thinner — Orodreth is a Ranger, not a Dwarf, and the app happily let him
lead a Dwarf-band anyway — so read this section as a systems check with a story painted
over it.

Six allies rolled in one tap: **Fár** the Quartermaster, **Blóvur** with the green thumb,
**Bári** who is *Shadow-Touched*, two **Munin**s and a second **Blóvur** *(the name roller
does not check for duplicates)*. Mission objective, rolled: *"Reclaim an important
landmark."* Medium band, geared for war, Sentinels.

> *"🗺️ Mission setup applied. Readiness 4 (TN 16) · Burden heavy — Dispositions: Exp 2,
> Man 2, Rally 2, Vig 3, War 3 — Eye Awareness 4 · Hunt Threshold 12"*

A Vigilance roll for the Band: `4 2 6 6 18 — Vigilance (Band) vs Readiness TN 16 — SUCCESS, 2 ✦`.
An Endurance Test against a *vicious* damage threat: `4 4 3 11 — Endurance Test (Rally,
vicious) vs Readiness TN 18 — FAIL — Fár suffers a FLEETING injury.`

Then the **Battle** tab, which is the app's most ambitious piece of machinery and which
worked. War Party rolled: **Warband — Might 2, Resistance 9**, with a **Greater Archfoe**
(−1d) and an Objective of 3. Get-in-Position succeeded and banked an Advantage called
*Upper Hand*. Ten Clash rounds followed, each one a Leader Focus roll, a Band Stance
choice, a Clash roll, and then successes spent:

> `R2 Leader Focus — Fight (Spears vs TN 14) SUCCESS: −1 foe Resistance, +1d Clash (opening)`
> `R3 Clash (War 3d) — FAIL; Endurance Test failed`
> `R5 Leader Focus — Fight (Spears) SUCCESS (Rune): −1 foe Resistance, +1d Clash (opening)`
> `R9 Leader Focus — Fight (Spears) FAIL: you lose 6 Endurance`
> `R10 🏆 VICTORY — the foe's Resistance is broken!`

> *"🏆 VICTORY! The War Party is defeated. Survivors are slain or flee (ask the Telling
> table if unsure). Award milestone XP — 'Survive a dangerous battle' (1 AP)."*

Five of the six allies came out of it with fleeting injuries. Aggressive stance is
Ill-Favoured and it shows: three of the ten Clashes failed outright, and each failure
dragged an Endurance Test behind it that also failed.

Switching Moria back off returned the +5 Hope cleanly, as advertised.

## XI. The ending

Back in the North, and the reckoning. Orodreth had come home from the Weather Hills having
saved nobody, carrying a ring that was colder than iron ought to be, and the Shadow found
the gap:

> **⚠️ BOUT OF MADNESS** — *"Shadow (10 + 1 Scar) reached Max Hope (11). Shadow Path: 'Path
> of Despair' (0/4 Flaws acquired). Pick a Flaw to add"* → **Guilt-ridden**

Before that, **🔥 Harden Will**, which is the app's honest little devil's bargain:

> *"Clear all 3 current Shadow → gain 1 permanent Shadow Scar (now 0 → 1). A Scar counts as
> Shadow for Miserable / Bout of Madness, but can only be removed by the Heal Scars
> undertaking at Yule (5 AP per Scar)."*

And the wound, rolled properly when I ticked **Wounded**:

> *"Wound Severity: **Severe Injury (7)** — 7 days to mend; dies in 1 hr without Healing roll"*

then First Aid, which reduces by `1 + ✦`:

> `Severe Injury — 5 days to mend (First Aid: 7→5)`

I wrote the last of it into the **Chronicle**, which had been filling itself the whole time
without my help — 51 auto-captured entries in the first scene alone, with the fight folded
into one collapsible heading:

> `⚔️ Combat vs Orc Soldier, Orc Guard — 2 foes · 1 slain · 4 rounds · −26 End · -2 Hope`

and my own paragraph beneath it:

> *Gilraen will want the truth, and the truth is thin. The refuge stands; the Ranger in it
> will not leave it. Orodreth came back down the Greenway with a dead orc behind him, a ring
> in his pack that is colder than iron ought to be, and the strong sense that something
> followed him as far as the Chetwood and then chose not to.*

The Chronicle exported as Markdown — 4,954 bytes, titled `# Orodreth — Tale of Years`, every
roll and oracle answer in order under its scene heading.

The saga's ending was the last thing, and the app would not let me have it until it decided
I had earned it *(Finding 8)*. With Valour at 5 the card finally offered:

> *"**This could be an ending.** • Valour 5 / Wisdom 1 — your hero is near the height of
> what they can become. Stopping well is part of playing well — a story that ends is better
> than one that fades."*

> **🏁 End your saga** — *"How does your hero's story end? ... Write the last line."*

> *He gave the cold ring to Gilraen, kept the refuge's name out of his report, and walked
> north until the Weather Hills were behind him for good.*

> *"Orodreth's tale is finished. Export the Chronicle from that tab to keep it. Your hero
> stays in the roster — nothing is deleted."*

**Final sheet:** Orodreth, Ranger of the North, Warden · Valour 5 · Wisdom 1 ·
Endurance 20/26 · Hope 11/11 · 1 Shadow Scar · Flaw: Guilt-ridden · Wounded (5 days) ·
6 Treasure · one cursed Númenórean ring · 2 sessions · 2 adventures · ended.

# Findings

Ranked by how much they got in the way of playing. Every quotation is the app's own text.

### 1 — The ▶ Play tab dead-ends when you travel on the Journey tab *(dead end in the fiction)*

**Beat:** §III→§IV, arriving at the refuge.

The Play tab's step 2 and the Character tab's adventure-loop card both send you to the
Journey tab (*"Do now: Open the Journey tab, fill it in, and tap ▶ Start Journey"*). When
you then finish the journey there — the app itself says *"Journey complete!"* — the Play tab
still reads:

> **ON THE ROAD TO THE RANGER REFUGE IN THE WEATHER HILLS** —
> *"You are ready to travel, but have not set out yet."*

…and offers only **🥾 Set out on the road** and **↩ Go back to the haven**. The button that
advances the game, **🏁 We have arrived**, is rendered only while `char.journey.active` is
true — which arriving has just cleared. There is no way to reach step 3 from the Play tab.

This matters because the Play tab's own intro promises *"You never need the other tabs to
play a whole session."* Recovery exists but is not signposted: the **▶ We have arrived**
button on the Character tab's adventure-loop card.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:3524` — `_playChoices()`, `case 'journey'`, the
`char.journey.active` ternary.

### 2 — A journey event tells you to roll and gives you nothing to roll with *(described capability, no control)*

**Beat:** §III, the Short Cut on the road east.

> `🎲 Short Cut (Feat 9, region wild). Roll: Awareness. +1 Travel Fatigue.`
> `If the skill roll succeeds: −1 day to journey`
> `Helpful tracks — follow the tracks`
> `▶ Roll Awareness.`

The Journey tab's log contains **no control at all** — the whole `#journey-log-card`
contains zero buttons. You must leave for the Dice tab, find the named skill yourself, roll
it, and then come back to a tab that has no field in which to record the result and no way
to apply the *"−1 day to journey"* it just promised. Every other subsystem in the app
(Council, Endeavour, Battle, Encounter) rolls its own skill in place and applies the effect.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:2502-2504` — `playerHint` is rendered as text only; nothing
consumes `targetSkill`.

### 3 — Enabling Strider Mode silently deletes the Prowess virtue's bonus *(silent state change)*

**Beat:** §I→§II, and it cost a real roll in §IV.

`toggleStriderMode()` recomputes every Attribute TN as a flat `18 − Rating` and does not
re-apply `char.prowessAttr`. After the switch, Orodreth's sheet still listed
`Prowess — Lower one Attribute TN by 1` and `char.prowessAttr === 'wit'`, but his Wits TN
was 14 where it should have been 13. Nothing told me. The failed Scan in §IV
(`Scan roll 9 vs 14`) and the failed Stealth (`7 vs 14`) were both rolled against the wrong
number. The same applies to switching Strider Mode back off.

**Cause:** `src/01-core.js:198-200`.

### 4 — Dying is the most consequential state in the game and the app never explains it *(needed the rulebook)*

**Beat:** §V, `⚠ you are Dying`.

At 0 Endurance the app shows a **DYING** pill on the Character tab and prints `⚠ you are
Dying` in the combat line. That is the entirety of what it ever says. Searching the whole
`REFERENCE` object produces exactly one hit, a subordinate clause inside the *Endurance*
definition (*"At 0 you are Dying."*). There is no `data-hint="Dying"` anywhere in the
document, no Reference entry, no timer, no prompt, and no control. The ▶ Play tab — the
surface designed to run the session for a newcomer — displayed `Endurance 0/26` in its
vitals line and offered the identical five choices it had offered at full health.

A Prolonged Rest then cleared it in one tap (*"Endurance: +26 → 26 / 26"*) without ever
naming what had been cleared.

### 5 — The Play tab and the Journey tab measure the same journey in incompatible units *(silent inconsistency)*

**Beat:** §III.

`playSetOut()` offers *"Close by — a day or two" / "A fair way — several days" / "Far — a
long road"* and sets `totalHexes` to 2 / 4 / 7. `playTravel()` then advances 2 hexes on a
success. But `applyMarchingTestResult()` on the Journey tab advances `3 + icons` on a
success — so one Marching Test consumed an entire *"several days"* journey in a single
press, and the Play tab's four-beat road became one. Either surface alone is coherent;
together they are not, and the app routes you between them.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:3633` (`playSetOut`) vs `src/04-render.js:1472-1478`.

### 6 — The Fellowship Phase wizard promises to preserve state it discards *(a lie in the record)*

**Beat:** §IX, step 3.

> *"Tap Close, use Spend XP modals on Character tab, then re-open this wizard to continue.
> Close this wizard temporarily to spend XP, then re-open to continue with Undertakings.
> **(Phase state is preserved.)**"*

`openFPWizard()` rebuilds `fpState` from scratch every time: `step: 1`, `phaseType: null`,
`recoveryApplied: false`, `selectedUndertakings: []`. Following the instruction on screen
throws away your phase type and your applied Recovery, and returns you to step 1.

Worse, because `recoveryApplied` resets while the Hope and Shadow changes have already
landed on the character, **Recovery can be applied more than once in the same Fellowship
Phase**. I did this by accident: −1 Shadow on one pass (`Shadow: 4 → 3`) and −2 on the next
(`3 → 1`), inside a single phase.

Step 3 also has no Spend control of its own — the step whose job is spending XP is a
signpost to another tab.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:353-370`.

### 7 — Clash successes become unspendable if the page reloads mid-round *(a stall)*

**Beat:** §X, the Battle.

`clashRoll()` writes the successes you rolled to `char.battle._pendingSpend`, which is
saved and survives a reload — but the panel that spends them is injected by
`renderClashSpend()`, which is called **only** from `clashRoll()` and `clashSpend()`, never
from `renderBattle()`. I reproduced it: after a reload, `char.battle._pendingSpend === 1`
and the number of visible `clashSpend` controls was **0**. The four buttons that could
spend it (`⚔ −1 Foe Resistance`, `🎯 −1 Objective`, `▲ Gain Advantage`, `☠ Harry Archfoe`)
are simply gone, and rolling another Clash overwrites the pending count rather than adding
to it. On a phone PWA, backgrounding the app mid-round is enough to lose a Clash.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:1691` `renderClashSpend()` is not called by `renderBattle()`.

### 8 — You cannot choose to end your saga *(described capability, no control)*

**Beat:** §XI.

`sagaEnd()` has exactly one call site, and it sits inside a block that renders only when
`sagaEndSignals()` returns something. Its own dialog text explicitly offers *"or they
simply **stop**, because the tale you wanted to tell is told"* — but there is no control to
do that until the app decides you have earned an ending.

The signals themselves are barely reachable. Valour or Wisdom ≥ 5 is many sessions away.
The Shadow signal (`Shadow + Scars ≥ Max Hope`) is **self-erasing**: the Bout of Madness
fires on exactly the same threshold and clears Shadow to 0 in the same breath, so by the
time you look at the saga card the signal is gone. I only reached an ending by pushing
Valour to 5 with the counter.

### 9 — The session recap shows the three *oldest* events in the campaign, labelled "Last time" *(a lie in the record)*

**Beat:** §XI, starting session 2.

> **📖 Session start** — *"Session 2. **Last time:** • Saga begins: A Ranger has gone
> missing. What has become of them? • Gained 3 Shadow (now 3). • Gained 1 Shadow (now 4)."*

`char.timeline` is stored newest-first, and `sagaStartSession()` does
`(char.timeline || []).slice(-3).reverse()` — which takes the last three of a newest-first
array, i.e. the three *oldest* entries. Every session start for the rest of the campaign
will recap the first three things that ever happened.

Compounding it, the timeline only captures Shadow gains, rank-ups and Fellowship Phases —
so even correctly ordered, a session containing a battle, a rescue, a failed Council, a
Revelation Episode and a Fellowship Phase recaps as two Shadow gains.

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:3276`.

### 10 — 🌑 Shadow Tests are unreachable until you make an unrelated roll *(a stall)*

**Beat:** §VIII.

The `🌑 Shadow Tests` card — the only in-app way to resist incoming Shadow, and a thing the
Character tab and Reference both send you to — is a child of `#roll-result`, which ships
`display:none` and is only revealed when a roll is made. On a freshly loaded Dice tab the
Dread / Greed / Sorcery buttons have a bounding box of 0×0. I had to roll something
unrelated first before the card existed. The `Combat Tasks` card and the Strider
`Special Success` panel are nested the same way.

`#shadow-test-card` has no `display:none` of its own; it is hidden purely by inheritance.

**Cause:** `character-tracker.html:1433` (`#roll-result` `display:none`) enclosing
`:1471` (`#shadow-test-card`).

### 11 — "The road home" cannot be travelled *(dead end)*

**Beat:** §XI.

Play step 4 offers **🥾 Travel onward — *Same as the road out.*** But `playArrive()` and the
adventure loop have cleared `char.journey`, so the button answers, every single time:

> *"You are not travelling yet."*

There is no *Set out* option at this step, so the only way forward is **🏠 We are safe

**Cause:** `src/04-render.js:3539-3542` — `_playChoices()`, `case 'home'`.

### 12 — The Eye of Mordor's threshold hint is stale and states a wrong number *(a lie in the record)*

### 13 — The Moria dialog promises a Patron it never gives you *(described capability, no control)*

# Not covered

- **The Loremaster / GM tab, campaigns, party view, Table Mode and anything cloud-backed.**
  Solo scope by design, and the harness blocks the Firebase SDK.
- **Group play of any kind** — Support given by a real ally, Fellowship Focus, shared
  encounters, broadcast.
- **The interactive Tutorial** (10 lessons / 57 steps) and its practice-hero sandbox.
- **Yule**, and therefore Heal Scars, Raise an Heir and Recount a Story.
- **The Duel** against a Moria Archfoe (the app hands this off to the Combat tab by design)
  and the Moria journey/chamber/orc-band generators.
- **Pre-generated heroes** — the picker was opened and its 13 entries read, but none was
  loaded; the hero was built by hand.
- **Random Lifepath**, the Treasure Index picker, Famous Weapon dormant qualities, and
  Cursed-item *Shadow Taint* applied through a Fellowship Phase.
- **Multi-character roster, share links / QR, import-export of characters, Restore Points,
  print, themes and text size.**
- **Whether the fiction the oracles produced was any good.** I can say the Telling and Lore
  tables and the Patron Quest converged on a coherent mission in §II without any steering
  from me, which is the best thing that happened all session. I cannot say that generalises.
- **Long-run balance.** Two sessions, two adventures, one Fellowship Phase. Nothing here
  tests whether advancement, Eye Awareness or Shadow pace correctly over a campaign.
