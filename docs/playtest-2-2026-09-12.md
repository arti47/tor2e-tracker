# Duinhir 'Eaglenose' and the silence below Weathertop

**A second solo playtest of the TOR2E Tracker — 2026-09-12.**
Played through a Playwright driver that presses controls by their visible label, one beat per
invocation, state on disk between beats. Every die result quoted below is copied verbatim from the
app. Seeded PRNG (`SEED=4747`, advanced once per invocation). Zero console errors in ~300
invocations.

Run 1 built the hero by hand. This time I did what the app's own front door invites: took a
**pre-generated hero** in one tap, turned on **Strider Mode**, and played.

---

## Prologue — the tutorial, and the practice hero that nearly ate my character

A fresh install offers, after a beat:

> **📖 Welcome to the TOR2E Tracker** — *"This app plays The One Ring 2nd Edition — solo or with a
> group. It does the rules and the maths for you. Never played before? The guided tutorial runs on
> a throwaway practice hero and teaches the whole game…"*
> `[📖 Start the tutorial] [Not now]`

I took it. Ten lessons, and the whole thing runs on a sandbox hero called **Beran (Practice)** so
your own sheet is untouched. I walked *How the Game Works* end to end, then *Dice Basics*, where
step 1 says "Choose a number of Success dice and tap 🎲 Roll Dice. Try it now" and offers
**👉 Try it on the page →**. The tutorial steps aside, leaves a **↩ Return to tutorial** pill
floating, and when I actually pressed **🎲 Roll Dice** — `7 / 5 → 12 vs TN 15 — FAIL` — it flashed
a tick and advanced itself to step 2 without my asking. That is the best-built thing in the app.

Then I closed the browser mid-lesson, the way anyone does. On the next boot:

> **📖 Tutorial was still open** — *"You closed the app while the tutorial was open, so you are
> currently on its practice hero — Practice Hero. Your own character New Hero is safe in your
> roster. Choose next whether to keep the practice hero or discard it."*

That recovery is exactly right, and it worked in both directions: discarding deleted the practice
hero and put my real one back; keeping renamed *Beran (Practice)* to *Beran* and left both in the
roster. The one wrong note is the choice itself, which arrives as a bare **`[OK] [Cancel]`** on the
sentence *"Save Beran (Practice) as a real character, or discard it."* Two destructive-ish outcomes,
two generic labels.

## I — One tap for a hero

☰ → **✨ Pre-generated Heroes — play in one tap** → **Duinhir 'Eaglenose' · Rangers of the North ·
Warden · Str 6 Hrt 5 Wit 4 · End 26 Hope 11.** Long Sword (Grievous), Spear, the virtue
*Dour-handed*, features *Honourable, Stern, Shadow-Lore*, and a Patron: **Gilraen, daughter of
Dírhael**.

☰ → **🗡️ Solo Play: Strider Mode**, which explains itself properly before it changes anything —
*"In Strider Mode you play both parts: you act as your hero, and you ask the Oracle whenever you
don't know what the world does."* TNs dropped 14/15/16 → **12/13/14**, experience quietly switched
to Milestone, and a free *Strider* feature appeared.

I gave him **Prowess** from the Build tab and lowered his Wits TN. Toggling Strider off and on
again: `20−6, 20−5, 20−4−1 = 14/15/15` → `18−6, 18−5, 18−4−1 = 12/13/13`. The virtue survives the
round trip, which is the thing that was broken last time.

Then I went to the Oracle to ask his Patron what she wanted of him, and the app said:

> **No Patron** — *"Set your Patron on the Build tab first (Strider Mode supplement quests are
> available for the 6 Core Rules patrons)."*

His sheet says Gilraen. The quest table's key is `'Gilraen, daughter of Dirhael'`; the pregen
stores `'Gilraen, daughter of Dírhael'`. One accent, and the Patron on the sheet is a stranger to
the rest of the app. **All five** Player-Heroes pregens carry that same string.

So I asked the world instead. **Lore Table → `Defend / Good / Fellowship`.** Then the Telling
Table:

> *Q: Does Gilraen's summons name a place Duinhir already knows?*
> **Feat die: 7 · chance: middling (yes if ≥ 6) → YES**

Good. A place he knows. I wrote the premise and began the saga:

> *"Gilraen has sent word that the steading below Weathertop, which Duinhir warded as a young man,
> has gone silent."*

## II — At the haven

The ▶ Play tab opens on the game, not the sheet, and states the whole loop in three lines. I asked
around for news:

> *"You listen at the fire and in the doorways. What you hear turns on three things:*
> **Demand · Desolate · Fate**.*"*

A demand made in a desolate place. Something took tribute from the steading and the steading is
gone. He puts on his cloak.

## III — The road east

**🥾 Set out on the road** → *"The silent steading below Weathertop"* → **A fair way — several
days** = 9 stretches. Then, pressing only Play:

> *The going is hard and slow. (Travel roll 11 vs 13 — failure.) 2 stretches in 2 days.*
> *The going is hard and slow. (Travel roll 9 vs 13 — failure.) 2 stretches in 2 days.*

And nothing else. Twice the app had scheduled a Journey Event at his current hex, and twice the
▶ Play tab — whose first line promises *"You never need the other tabs to play a whole session"* —
offered the same three buttons and never mentioned it. I had to go to the Journey tab to find the
road had been waiting to happen to him.

There it was, immediately:

> **🎲 Despair (Feat 1, region wild). Roll: Explore. +2 Travel Fatigue.** *If the skill roll fails:
> every hero present gains +1 Shadow (Dread).* *Corrupted site* — find your way out. Sub-event roll:
> 5 · Skill: Explore. **▶ Roll Explore.**

and this time the app brought the die with it — a proper card reading **"▶ Despair — roll Explore ·
Explore ◆◆◆◇◇◇ vs TN 13 · On a success: no Shadow from this"** with `🎲 Roll Explore` and
`Skip this roll`. That is a real fix and it is exactly what was missing last year.

> **▶ Explore roll for Despair — 10 vs TN 13: failure — the event's effect stands.**

Except it didn't. The +2 Travel Fatigue landed; the +1 Shadow the event had just promised in bold
never did, and nothing offered to apply it. Duinhir walked out of a corrupted barrow-site with his
Hope untouched.

Further on, **Ill Choices** (Feat 2) — *disorienting environs* — and this time
**"▶ Explore roll for Ill Choices — 16 vs TN 13: success — you keep your head — no Shadow gained."**
Then a **Mishap** at the last hex, beaten with `20 vs TN 13, 1 ✦`.

> **🏁 Arrived at The silent steading below Weathertop!** Travel Fatigue accumulated: **6**.
> Arrival TRAVEL roll: **FAIL** (Feat 2, total 12 vs Heart TN 13) → no reduction. Lingering **6**
> Fatigue added to character Fatigue (0 → 6).

Nine days on the road and six Fatigue riding on him. I went back to ▶ Play to see whether it had
noticed, and it had:

> *"The road is behind you — you finished this journey on the Journey tab. Tap **We have arrived**
> to carry on."*

Last year that was a dead end. This year it is a sentence and a button, and the button runs the
real arrival.

## IV — The steading

> *"You take the place in. What stands out: **Threaten · Unnatural · Trust**."*

Something unnatural, wearing a face the household trusted. Duinhir asked the only question that
mattered, phrasing it the way the app tells you to — so that yes is what he wants:

> *You wonder: Are any of the steading's folk still alive?* **The answer is NO.**

He searched anyway. **🔍 Search the place — Scan roll 9 vs 13: it doesn't. (1 ✦)** — a failure the
app refuses to let you shrug off: *"Say what goes wrong. A failure should cost something or change
the situation — it is not just 'nothing happens'."* What goes wrong is that he finds the barrow
door open, and while he is stooped over it, the thing inside notices him.

## V — The fight at the barrow door

Three foes: a **Barrow-wight** (End 20, Parry 5, Armour 2, Might 2) and two **Forgotten Dead**
(End 12, Parry 1, Armour 2). The Forgotten Dead's stat block includes *"Thing of Terror: Start of
round 1: all heroes gain 3 Shadow (Dread); fail blocks Hope spending."* Nothing in the app ever
applies that, or asks you to.

Duinhir went **Forward** and tried to break the wight's nerve first:

> **Intimidate Foe (Awe) · vs TN 12 — FAIL** *(`10 / 1 → 11`)*

Then steel. **You · Long Sword · 13 vs TN 17 (12 Str + Parry 5) → FAIL · miss.** Then
**17 vs TN 17 → SUCCESS (1✦) · −6 End → 14/20.** Forward stance is a trade, and the dead collected
on it:

> Forgotten Dead · Rusted Sword · 25 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −4 End → 18/26
> Forgotten Dead · Rusted Sword · 25 vs your Parry 18 · you Forward +1d → HIT · −4 End → 14/26 ·
> **Piercing Blow — Protection 11 vs 15 → WOUNDED (Severe Injury (4))**
> *Wound Severity: Severe Injury (4) — 4 days to mend; dies in 1 hr without Healing roll*

He dropped to **Open** and went at the wight until it fell —
`18 → 8/20`, `17 → 2/20`, miss, `17 vs TN 17 → SUCCESS (1✦) · −6 End → 0/20 · ⚔ Barrow-wight slain!`

Bleeding, outnumbered two to one, he tried to break off. **🏃 Fly, You Fools!** explains the two
RAW ways out and then asks you to *type* `1` or `2` into a text box. (I typed `banana` first, out
of curiosity; the app did nothing and said nothing.) I typed `2`, took Defensive, and rolled the
escape where it told me to — the Dice tab:

> `5 / 3 / 2 → 10` · **Swords · vs TN 12 — FAIL** · Modifier: [Def −2d] · ✨ Hope spent (+1d)

TN **12** — because the Dice tab does not know which foe he is disengaging from, while the same
swing on the Combat tab was TN **17**. He failed either way, and the dead closed back in
(`19 vs your Parry 18 → HIT · −4 End → 10/26`).

So he stopped running. A Gandalf rune ended it:

> **You · Long Sword · ★ vs TN 13 (12 Str + Parry 1) → SUCCESS (Rune) · −6 End → 6/12 · Piercing
> Blow — foe Protection 14 vs 16 → foe WOUNDED**

and two swings later, `22 vs TN 13 → SUCCESS (1✦) · −6 End → 0/12 · ⚔ Forgotten Dead slain!`

The Chronicle folded the whole thing into one collapsible heading, which is the single most
pleasing thing the journal does:

> **⚔️ Combat vs Barrow-wight, Forgotten Dead — 3 foes · 3 slain · 9 rounds · −16 End · Wounded**

Afterwards, **First Aid (Healing) — ★ vs 13 → SUCCESS (Rune!)** took the Severe Injury from 4 days
to 2, and a Prolonged Rest —*"A new day dawns (Day 2). Endurance: +6 → 16 / 26 · Injury: 2 → 1"*—
gave him a night. A Short Rest had refused, correctly: *"☀️ Short Rest while Wounded: no Endurance
recovered (Core Rules p.71)."*

## VI — What was in the barrow

A Greater hoard, tainted. **Treasure rolls: 5, 3 = 8 per hero.** And in it a blade. I used the
**📖 Treasure Index**, which pre-filled every field from Andúril and then let me rename it, because
what he found was not Andúril:

> ✨ Famous Weapon **"The Barrow-blade of Cardolan"** added. Load: +1. ⚠️ CURSED (Shadow Taint).
> You will gain +1 Shadow each Fellowship Phase while bearing this item.

Three qualities, one awake: *Superior Fell* **ACTIVE**, *Foe-Slaying* and *Cleaving* **DORMANT**.
He should not have taken it, and he knows it.

One thing the Index cannot give him: his own Long Sword's Notes read *"Injury 18 when used
2-handed"*, and there is no grip control on a pre-generated hero's weapons at all. Only weapons
picked from the catalogue get one — I proved it by picking a Spear from the list, which came with a
`1h`/`2h` toggle that duly swapped Injury 14 → 16.

## VII — The road home, and the Bree-folk

**✅ Our business here is done** → a red banner I had not seen before:

> **⚠️ TAKE CARE** — *"You are Wounded. A Wound does not heal with an ordinary rest — it needs
> treatment and time. Wounded and at 0 Endurance is how heroes actually die."*
> `[🩹 Tend the wound] [🥾 Set out for home] [🏠 We are safe again]`

**🥾 Set out for home** now actually sets out — *"The way we came"*, nine stretches back — where
last year it answered "You are not travelling yet" forever. Four presses (`12 vs 13 — failure`,
`7 vs 13 — failure`, `10 vs 13 — failure`, `16 vs 13 — success`) and *"The place you were making
for is in sight."*

He carried 24 Load and 5 Fatigue against 16 Endurance. The Character tab showed a pulsing **WEARY**
pill. The ▶ Play tab's vitals line — the only line a Play-only player ever reads — said
`Endurance 16/26 · Hope 10/11 · Shadow 0 · 👁 0 · WOUNDED` and nothing about being Weary.

At Bree he called a Council: *close the Weathertop road until the barrows are cleansed.* Bold (6),
audience **Reluctant −1d**. The Introduction went badly — **Awe: Feat 6, total 10 vs STR TN 12 →
Time Limit set to 3** — three attempts to move a hostile room:

> Persuade — Feat 6, total 6 vs WIT TN 13. No contribution.
> Enhearten — Feat 10, total 13 vs HRT TN 13. **Contributed +1.**
> Riddle — **Feat 👁**, total 0 vs WIT TN 13. No contribution.

> ⏳ *Time limit reached. 1 / 6 successes.* `[Accept Failure] [Success with Woe]`

He took the Woe. The road closes; the Rangers will pay Bree for the lost trade, and Duinhir will
hear about it for years. (The Eye of Sauron on that last roll raised nothing: Eye Awareness stayed
at 3. The Eye only counts on the Dice tab.)

Then a **Skill Endeavour** in the Cardolan barrows — Laborious (6), four attempts, Hazardous — and
the dice finally turned: `14`, **`★` (rune)**, **`★` 1✦**, `18`. Five successes of six with the time
gone: *"❌ Time limit reached on a Hazardous endeavour. 5 / 6 successes."* So near.

## VIII — Yule, and the phase that will not end

Session closed — and here solo play is honest with you: *"Playing solo, award experience by
Milestone rather than by session — sessions vary too much in length for session rewards to track
what your hero actually achieved."* (Though the confirm one screen earlier had promised *"You will
be awarded the session's experience"*.)

I banked Milestones, took a Dread test (`2 / 4 → 6 vs TN 13 — FAIL`, *"No reduction — full Shadow
gain applies"*), applied 3 Shadow, and burned it out with **Harden Will** — Shadow 3 → 0, one
permanent Scar.

Then the **Fellowship Phase wizard**, at Yule.

Step 2 was everything the last report asked for. *"Hope Recovery: Full Hope restoration (Yule)…
❄️ Yule extras: Age 30 → 31 · Bonus Skill Points: +4 (your WITS rating)"*, and in red:
*"⚠️ Shadow Taint: 1 cursed item (The Barrow-blade of Cardolan) → +1 Shadow this phase."* I closed
the wizard mid-phase and reopened it: it came back **on step 2, with Yule still selected and
Recovery still applied**, and refused to apply Recovery twice. Both halves of last year's worst
lie are fixed.

Step 3 is where it fell over. The card says, in as many words:

> *"Spend from right here — **the wizard stays open underneath**."*
> `[Spend Skill Points] [Spend Adventure Points]`

It does not stay underneath. Both overlays carry `z-index:200`, and the wizard comes later in the
document, so it paints **on top of the modal it just opened**. I hit-tested every one of the Spend
XP modal's 19 buttons: **none of them is clickable.** The only way to spend experience in a
Fellowship Phase is to close the wizard — at which point `char.fpModeActive` goes false and the
Spend XP modal greets you with:

> *"Spending outside Fellowship Phase (no caps enforced; per RAW XP is only spent in FP — use the
> FP wizard for rule-correct play)."*

…which is what the wizard just told you to do. So I raised **Valour to 2 and Wisdom to 2 in the
same phase**, which the wizard's own step 3 prints as forbidden. Both rank-ups fired their pickers
— *"You've gained a new Valour rank — choose a Reward!"*, and a Wisdom picker that correctly opened
up the **Rangers of the North** cultural virtues (Endurance of the Ranger, Foresight of Their
Kindred, Heir of Arnor, Royalty Revealed, Strength of Will, Ways of the Wild). And because each
picker only exists on the rank-up path, when the moment passed I could not get either back:
16 Adventure Points spent, one Reward and one Virtue owed, and no control anywhere that will pay
them.

Undertakings worked — **Heal Scars** took the Scar and 5 AP — and completion reset the Eye:
*"Eye Awareness reset to starting value: 3. +2 culture, +0 Valour ≥ 4, +1 Famous item(s)."*

And then the phase would not end. `fpComplete()` clears the saved wizard state on its first line
and then calls `fpClose()`, which writes it straight back. Every Fellowship Phase after the first
opens **already in progress, with Recovery already applied**, and answers:

> *"Recovery already applied this session. Cancel and reopen to redo."*

Cancelling and reopening does not redo it — I checked, twice, across reloads. There is no
"start a new phase" control; `openFPWizard(forceNew)` exists and nothing calls it. Duinhir's Hope
and Shadow can never again be touched by a Fellowship Phase. Meanwhile each press of Complete runs
another Yule on the Tale of Years: the calendar rolled 2965 → **2970** while the hero aged one year.

The generators are good. **Chamber:** *Natural Civic Building — Goblin-gnawed — Challenge: None

**Moria Lore (Feature column):** *Enchantment.* **Fortune:** *You gain favoured ground.*

The battle went badly and instructively. Get-in-Position gave an Advantage (*Upper Hand*); a

Two Solo Tools closed the digression properly. **🎲 Hero or Band?** answers who the outcome lands

> 🛡️ **Hór** makes a Desperate Stand (Favoured & Inspired). Feat dice: **8 · 7**.
> **Hór is lost, having given everything for their fellows.** 🌑 +2 Shadow.

No Eye on either die. He does not come back. I made Fáin **Hardened** and gave him the cursed
barrow-blade as a **👑 Kingly Gift** — let it be someone else's weight — and a **Brief** Moria

−2 Shadow · Cleared 1 ally condition(s)."* Five Hope, full Heart — the Rangers' printed half-Heart
weakness, which the core wizard now honours ("+½ HEART (3 of 5) — Kings of Men weakness"), does not

I pushed the Eye to its threshold to see what waits there:

> **👁️ Moria Revelation — Dire Portents (Feat 6): Horror of Death.** *You find a corpse. Gain 2

# Findings

Ranked most disabling first.

### 1 — The Fellowship Phase can only be run once. Every phase after the first refuses Spiritual Recovery, permanently. *(stall)*
**Beat VIII.** `fpComplete()` begins with `char.fpWizardState = null;  // the phase is over` and
then ends by calling `fpClose()` → `fpPersist()`, which writes the just-completed `fpState` back
with `inProgress: true`. So the next phase opens mid-flight with `recoveryApplied: true` and
answers every press of Apply Recovery with:

> *"Recovery already applied this session. Cancel and reopen to redo."*

Cancelling and reopening does **not** redo it (verified across two reloads: `recoveryApplied`
stays `true`). `openFPWizard(forceNew)` exists; **nothing calls it** — the only two call sites
(`src/04-render.js:3969`, `character-tracker.html:747`) pass no argument. There is no other route
to Fellowship Hope recovery or Shadow removal in the app. Last phase's undertakings also carry
over and re-fire (Heal Scars was re-attempted every time), and each completion runs another Yule on
the Tale of Years — the calendar went 2965 → 2970 while the hero aged one year.
`src/04-render.js:700` (the null) vs `:398–404` (`fpClose`/`fpPersist`).

**Clean reproduction**, from a hero that had never opened the wizard: one complete Ordinary phase
(Type → Apply Recovery → Next → Next → Complete, every dialog answered) ends with
`char.phasesCompleted: 1` **and** `char.fpWizardState = {step: 4, phaseType: 'ordinary',
recoveryApplied: true, inProgress: true}`. Spend three Hope, open the wizard again, press Apply
Recovery: *"Recovery already applied this session."* Hope stays at 8/11.

### 2 — Step 3 of the Fellowship Phase opens the Spend XP modal *behind* itself. Zero of its 19 buttons can be clicked. *(stall)*
**Beat VIII.** The card says: *"Spend from right here — **the wizard stays open underneath**."*
Both overlays compute to `z-index: 200`; `fp-wizard-overlay` (`character-tracker.html:2217`) comes later in the document than `spend-xp-overlay` (`:1905`), so it
paints on top. Hit-testing `#spend-xp-overlay`'s buttons through `elementFromPoint` returns
`fp-wizard-overlay` at every sampled point; **reachable buttons: 0 of 19.** The workaround — close
the wizard — sets `char.fpModeActive = false` (`src/04-render.js:402`), so the only usable XP route
in a Fellowship Phase is the one that announces *"no caps enforced"*. I raised **Valour to 2 and
Wisdom to 2 in the same phase**, which the same card prints as forbidden.

### 3 — A Valour or Wisdom rank-up you don't spend immediately is lost forever. *(described capability, no control)*
**Beat VIII.** `openNewReward()` / `openNewVirtue()` are called from exactly one place
(`src/05-combat-build.js:2382` / `:2388`), inside the rank-up. Both pickers ship a **"Skip for
now"** button. Once dismissed — or once the app closes on the open picker — there is no control
anywhere in the document that reopens them (`[onclick*="openNewReward"]` → 0 elements). Duinhir
paid 16 AP for two ranks and received neither the Reward nor the Virtue.

### 4 — Journey Events are unreachable from the ▶ Play tab, which says you never need another tab. *(dead end in the fiction)*
**Beat III.** `playTravel()` advances through `applyMarchingTestResult()`, which sets
`journey.nextEventHex` — and then nothing in `renderPlay()` reads it. After two marching tests the
journey had an event due at the hero's own hex, and Play's situation line read
*"You have covered 4 of 9 stretches. Day 4."* with the same three buttons. A player who takes the
tab at its word (*"You never need the other tabs to play a whole session"*) never sees a single
Journey Event — the entire pressure mechanic of TOR2E travel. `playTravel` at `src/04-render.js:3834`; `renderPlay` at `:4034`.

### 5 — Clash successes are still unspendable after a reload; the fix moved the bug rather than removing it. *(stall / regressed fix)*
**Beat IX.** Run 1's fix added `renderClashSpend()` to `renderBattle()`, and it does run —
`#b-spend` holds 706 characters of markup. But `#b-spend` is a child of `#b-clash-result`, which is
`display:none` until `clashRoll()` or `clashSpend()` sets it, so after a reload the spend buttons
are rendered and **invisible** (`__vis → false`; not in the panel's visible-button list). The
banked success survives in `char.battle._pendingSpend`; the next Clash overwrites it. This is the
identical container-visibility pattern as run 1's finding 10 (Shadow Tests nested inside
`#roll-result`), which was fixed by moving the elements out — that fix was not applied here.

### 6 — A journey event announces a mechanical penalty, says it "stands", and never applies it. *(described capability, no control)*

### 7 — The Patron on five of the thirteen pre-generated heroes — and on every Moria hero — matches nothing in the app. *(described capability, no control)*
**Beats I and IX.** All five Player-Heroes pregens store `"Gilraen, daughter of Dírhael"` (í with
acute, `src/02-data.js:711, 882, 1044, 1205, 1371`); `PATRONS` and `PATRON_QUESTS` are keyed

Oracle tab for what they want"*) points at a refusal. `toggleMoriaMode` sets `char.patron = 'Balin'`,
which likewise matches neither table.

### 8 — The Lifepath Major Event's Parry change is erased on the next render. *(silent state change)*
**Beat X-adjacent (Build tab).** `MAJOR_EVENTS` die 9 — *"…vigorous: Increase Endurance by 2, lower
Parry rating by 1"* — applied End 25 → **27** and left Parry at **16**. `applyMajorEvent` writes

run-1 fix moved the Lifepath **TN** adjustments into `char.tnAdjust` (GOTCHA 19) and left the
Lifepath **Parry** adjustments as one-off writes to a derived value. (Nimbleness is safe — it
writes `parryBonusVirtue`, which is in the formula.)

  harness stubs the Firebase SDK, and the app correctly reports *"📴 Firebase SDK not loaded"*. The

  rolled per-hero against each hero's own TN (*"Beran Dread 2d vs 16 — FAIL (9) +1 Shadow"*). It is

  owner-only-write).
- **Combat breadth I still did not reach:** Opening Volley in an actual ranged volley (Duinhir has

  demanded tribute from a steading and wore a trusted face. The Telling Table then answered the one
  question that mattered with an inconvenient **NO**, and the session was better for it. I can say
  it worked this time. I cannot say it generalises.
